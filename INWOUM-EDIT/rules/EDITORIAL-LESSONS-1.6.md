# User update 1.6 — sales hooks and reviewable alternatives

Prioritize authentic sales Hooks from the ENTIRE source: audience pain, buyer motivation, curiosity,
emotional reaction, demonstration reveal, comparison and payoff. Never invent speech or stronger claims.
Sales value is not just new facts. A different buyer/problem/emotion can justify another version with
a shared body. Search late takes as carefully as early takes. Preserve qualitative value and negatives.

When EDITORIAL value is uncertain, retain a labelled alternative VERSION with a hook, supporting body,
payoff and reason for review. Do not discard it merely because another preferred version exists.
No quota, exhaustive permutations or bare-reaction microclips. Factual uncertainty remains labelled:
a variant does not verify a claim, remove caveats, or authorize invented facts/false demonstrations.

Evaluate source ROLES: Hook, problem, demonstration, reaction, feature, condition, value, buyer motivation,
transition and close. A reaction or reveal can sell a demo without adding new facts. Check neighboring
speech/video before calling it useless chatter; uncertain reactions can support a demo-led alternative.
Recover useful clauses inside mixed units. Do not discard a buyer-motivation clause because its tail
mentions another channel. Preserve setup/continuation across ASR segments, especially ไม่ใช่ว่า... .

FULL stays comprehensive <=180s after actual cuts. Plan ORDERED narrative beats: Hook/problem,
mechanism/demo, useful features, real conditions/value, payoff/close. Pre-cut time allocation is an
estimate, never verified output duration. Combine two topics only with a clear connection and payoff.
Reduce habitual แบบ; retain meaningful type/reference uses. No แต่/แต่ว่า/ก็/แบบ clip opening.
Infer intelligible minor ASR errors from context without spelling-repair loops or rewriting speech.
Keep original source audio/sync and all meaningful negation/uncertainty.
During step testing STOP at the requested checkpoint. Alternative versions remain plans until the
user authorizes assembling drafts/cuts. Never claim listening or visual approval.
