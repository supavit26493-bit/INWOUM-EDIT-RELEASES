# Effective update 1.5 — atomic facts and independent editing

FULL first and <=180 seconds after cuts. FULL must explain all distinct essential usable product facts,
not every retake. Keep useful weight, size, carry methods, actual mechanism/demo, use cases, limits and
qualitative/comparative price. Do not omit a useful new fact merely because one complete FULL exists.
Every usable clause must map to a fact with exact source options. Same fact in another take is an OPTION,
not a required extra release. A mixed unit's complete clauses remain usable even if its head/tail is bad.
Negation, uncertainty and genuine product-specific conditions must remain with the fact.
Unverified technical features may be kept as the speaker's claims without inventing verification;
unclear material guesses and unsupported numerical guarantees remain held with specific reasons.

All non-hold facts are checked against the final selected source words. full-scope facts belong in FULL;
collection-scope facts must occur in a focused story. Minimal evidence anchors must not force stutters,
abandoned openings or next-clause heads. Evidence does not replace a fluent complete released story.
Keep protected demonstrations and original audio/sync. Never claim successful listening/image review.

Workers share one inventory and an immutable input collection. FULL worker only writes FULL. Focused
worker only writes focused stories and compares that WHOLE collection for duplication/weak fragments.
Retain accepted strong stories; improve/add connected two-topic stories when useful. No target count,
minimum length, percentage usage or exhaustive permutations. A near-identical body needs a meaningful
different problem/audience/payoff to justify another release. New information need not become a new clip.
Do not preserve an unnecessary short price-only fragment simply because it existed before.
Use exact Thai source names in titles; do not invent English spellings. Reasons concise and factual.
