# Latest user instructions 1.4 — 21 October 2026

The accepted focused clips are good. Preserve their useful content and roles while improving FULL.
Every clip MUST start with an intelligible independent phrase. NEVER start with แต่, แต่ว่า, ก็ or แบบ.
Do not move a meaningless connector into a different first selection to evade this rule. Read the actual
assembled opening. Select a clean source suffix or another complete take; do not generate new speech.
The ban applies at clip openings, not to necessary internal contrast or conditions.

FULL must be fluent, comprehensive and <=180 seconds. Remove accidental exact word repeats (อีกอีก,
ซ่อนซ่อน, จริงจริง), stutters, abandoned premises and repeated explanations across different takes.
Read every selected phrase and every join as continuous speech. State each substantive fact once;
do not repeat product introduction, valuables list or travel premise merely to increase coverage.
Keep useful additional demonstrations, distinct facts, necessary qualifiers and negations.
Clean repetition by source ranges/another take, never by editing transcript text without editing sound.
Use source Thai names in titles when English spelling is unverified. Do not invent transliterations.

A focused/long_focused clip may combine TWO related topics when together they answer a stronger viewer
question with a coherent transition and complete ending (e.g. an actual problem + relevant mechanism).
content_group denotes the PRIMARY inventory group only; it does NOT restrict selections to that group.
Describe both topics and their relationship in distinct_body_summary and story_outline. There is no
one-group-per-clip rule. Also retain effective single-topic clips; do not manufacture every permutation,
fragment good stories or add near-duplicates to satisfy a count. Assess marginal usefulness honestly.

This update overrides older inferences requiring every focused edit to discuss exactly one group.
All original-audio, meaning, safety/price, sync and pending-listening rules still apply.
